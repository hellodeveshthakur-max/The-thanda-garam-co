ZERO PRE-MIX
THE REAL DEAL

Authentic slush.
No mixing, no shortcuts.

Most slush comes from a packet — pour, dilute, done. Ours doesn't. Every flavour is churned to order from real fruit and cold syrups, so what's spinning in the machine is exactly what lands in your cup. No colouring dumped in for effect, no "just add water."

  • 🍋
    Real fruit, real flavour

    Cola, lichi and mango, made from actual pulp and syrup — not flavouring powder.

  • 🚫
    Zero pre-mix packets

    Nothing arrives ready-made. Every batch is built fresh at the counter.

  • ❄️
    Churned, not just frozen

    Slow-churned to that proper barf-ka-gola texture — thick, never watery.

OUR VIBE

Thanda when it's hot.
Garam when it's not.

We're a little corner shop in New Krishna Park, Vikaspuri, built for the two moods everyone shows up with: cool it down, or warm it up. So the menu is split the same way — Thanda for slush, fruit cream and softy, Garam for churros, momos, fries and cigar rolls.

"The atmosphere was surreal — a quiet, peaceful and surprisingly uplifting environment made it a great place to even just sit down."

Everything on the menu is 100% vegetarian, made fresh behind the counter, and served in cups that are basically part of the outfit at this point.
